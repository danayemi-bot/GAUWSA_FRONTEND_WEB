About us

A student association built on unity and action

GAUWSA is the Garissa University Wailwana Students Association — a student-led network created to unite Wailwana students, amplify student voices, and create a stronger campus experience for everyone.

We work to strengthen belonging, encourage leadership, improve access to opportunities, and build meaningful partnerships that support academic success and personal growth.

Student advocacy Academic support Leadership development

Vision

To empower the Ilwana community through excellence, unity, and opportunity across every sphere of life.

Mission

To represent, support, and empower Wailwana students with practical programs, services, and leadership opportunities.
