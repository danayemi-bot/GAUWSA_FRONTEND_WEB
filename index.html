About us

Who are GAUWSA?

GAUWSA stands for Garissa University Wailwana Students Association. It is a student-led organization created to unite Wailwana students at Garissa University and provide a strong platform for advocacy, mentorship, and meaningful engagement.

We believe that solidarity and shared purpose create opportunity, and that every student deserves a voice, a sense of belonging, and the support needed to grow academically and socially.

Vision

To see the Ilwana community prosper in all sectors and improve the quality of life for every member.

Mission

To promote the welfare, development, and representation of Wailwana students at Garissa University and beyond.
